Learning Bhangra the Right Way

Bhangra has grown enormously around the world and today can be seen in competitions, stage performances, music videos and popular entertainment. As it has become more mainstream, Bhangra is also frequently blended with Bollywood, contemporary dance and other modern styles.

There is nothing wrong with enjoying different styles of dance, but parents specifically looking for their children to learn Bhangra should understand the difference.

Traditional Punjabi Folk Bhangra has its own movements, technique, rhythm, energy and cultural identity. At Perth Bhangra Academy, we believe young dancers should first develop a strong foundation in authentic Folk Bhangra.

Choosing a Bhangra Academy for Your Child

When comparing Bhangra or Punjabi dance classes in Perth, parents can consider what they actually want their child to learn.

If the goal is authentic Bhangra, look at the academy's teaching style, performances and competition teams. A good children's program should also provide an environment where students can progress gradually rather than feeling pressured to perform before they are ready.

About PBA

More Than Dance.
It’s A Way Of Life.

Perth Bhangra Academy is a leading Bhangra academy in Perth, Western Australia, dedicated to teaching and promoting authentic Punjabi folk Bhangra. Established in 2022, the academy provides Bhangra classes for kids, adults, women and competition teams in Piara Waters, Brabham, Canning Vale and across Perth.

More than dance, our focus is on preserving Punjabi culture while building confidence, discipline, leadership and community. Students are given opportunities to learn, perform and compete while proudly representing Punjabi folk culture on stage.
